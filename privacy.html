Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers Historical Henry Uploader, a private desktop tool that runs only on the owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner to publish videos to his own channels on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube API Services

Historical Henry Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

What data is accessed and how it is used

  • Authorized account data: with the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the tool uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel, creates playlists there and updates the descriptions of the owner's own videos. It reads the owner's channel name to confirm which channel is connected.
  • Public data: the tool reads public YouTube search results and public video statistics (titles, view counts, channel subscriber counts) only to decide which history topics to cover next. This data is never displayed, published or shared.
  • No personal data of other YouTube users is collected. No cookies or tracking are used.

Storage

  • The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on the owner's computer.
  • Public YouTube data used for topic research is stored in a local cache for at most 24 hours and then replaced. It is never kept longer than 30 days.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Data is only sent to YouTube (Google), TikTok and Instagram (Meta) as needed to publish the owner's own videos.

Deletion and revoking access

  • Access to YouTube can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also at myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working immediately. The owner then deletes the local token file. Cached public data is replaced automatically within 24 hours and can be deleted at any time.
  • To request deletion of any stored data, contact denciiiik1002@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 7 days.
  • TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services), Instagram access in Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites).

Other platforms

See the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy.
