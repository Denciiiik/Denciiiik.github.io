Privacy Policy

The upload tools listed on this site (for example Historical Henry Uploader) are private tools used only by their owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels (for example "The Historical Henry") through the YouTube Data API, the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram API.

The tools do not collect, store or share personal data of any other people. The access that the channel owner grants is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used solely to upload and manage videos on the owner's channels.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The only data processed is what is needed to publish the owner's videos: the access tokens of the owner's own accounts, the video files and their titles and descriptions. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Access can also be revoked in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Apps and services) and in Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites).

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, as well as the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy.
